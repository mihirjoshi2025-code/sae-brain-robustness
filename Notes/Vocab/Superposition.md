# Superposition

**In one line:** the reason model internals are a mess — a single number carries a bit of many concepts at once ("dog" + "past tense" + "sad"), so you can't read it directly.

**Why it matters:** superposition is the problem [[Sparse Autoencoder (SAE)|SAEs]] exist to solve. Un-blending superposition into separate [[Feature|features]] is what makes interpretability possible.
