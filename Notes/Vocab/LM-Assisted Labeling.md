# LM-Assisted Labeling

**In one line:** using an LLM (e.g., GPT-4) to **label what each [[Sparse Autoencoder (SAE)|SAE]] [[Feature|feature]] detects**, instead of a human reading thousands of features by hand.

**Why we care:** it's how the [[Feature Taxonomy|taxonomy]] scales — but it's also a risk. **An AI labeling an AI** may share blind spots; the human spot-check might miss them. So we validate a sample and report [[Inter-Rater Agreement (Kappa)|κ]].
