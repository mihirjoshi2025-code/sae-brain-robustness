# fMRI

**In one line:** a brain scanner that tracks **blood flow** (the BOLD signal) as a slow stand-in for neural activity while a person reads or listens.

**Key limitation:** it takes a snapshot every ~2 seconds, during which a person hears several words — so it **blurs words together** in time (see [[Hemodynamic Delay]]). Intracranial methods are faster (see [[Lit - Back to the Feature (ICLR 2026)]]).

**Our data:** [[LeBel-Huth fMRI|naturalistic stories]] and [[Pereira 2018|isolated sentences]], measured in [[Voxel|voxels]].
