# Pereira 2018

**In one line:** an [[fMRI]] dataset of **isolated sentences** (short, controlled stimuli) rather than continuous stories.

**Role in the grid:** the **short-context** dataset. Clean and controlled, but **few stimuli** → noisy, so some comparisons may not reach significance. **Always report [[Noise Ceiling|noise ceilings]].**

**Contrast with:** [[LeBel-Huth fMRI]] (long naturalistic stories).

**Related:** [[LeBel-Huth fMRI]] · [[Noise Ceiling]] · [[Experimental Grid]]
