# Word Rate

**In one line:** how many words occur per unit time — a **low-level** baseline predictor of brain activity.

**Why it's a control:** the brain responds to the *pace* of speech regardless of meaning. If a fancy feature set barely beats word rate, its "understanding" claim is weak.
