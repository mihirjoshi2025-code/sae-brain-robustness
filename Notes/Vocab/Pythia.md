# Pythia

**In one line:** an open family of LLMs released with **many training [[Checkpoint|checkpoints]]**, made for studying how models change during training.

**Why we care:** the main vehicle for the **stretch goal** — measure alignment/geometry across training steps without training anything ourselves.
