# Gemma Scope

**In one line:** a public set of [[Sparse Autoencoder (SAE)|SAEs]] trained on **Gemma 2**, covering many layers and [[Dictionary Size|sizes]].

**Why we care:** one of the candidate model+SAE pairings for the grid. Final model list depends on whether Gemma Scope covers the [[Layer|layers]] and sizes we need.
