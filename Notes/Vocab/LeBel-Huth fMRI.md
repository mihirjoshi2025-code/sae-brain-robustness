# LeBel-Huth fMRI

**In one line:** a large **naturalistic stories** [[fMRI]] dataset — subjects listen to many hours of podcasts/stories while scanned.

**Role in the grid:** the **long-context** dataset. Rich, continuous language → good [[Noise Ceiling|noise ceilings]], but confounded (lots of co-occurring structure).

**Contrast with:** [[Pereira 2018]] (short isolated sentences). A real effect should appear in **both**.
