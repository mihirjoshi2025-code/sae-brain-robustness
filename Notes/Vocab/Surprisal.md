# Surprisal

**In one line:** how *unexpected* the next word is, given the context — low surprisal = easy to guess, high surprisal = surprising.

**Double role in our project:**
- **Control:** meaning-based claims (2 & 3) must beat a surprisal-only baseline.
- **Claim 4:** frontal language regions may be explained by surprisal *alone*.

**Note:** surprisal estimates differ by model, so compute it consistently across configurations.
