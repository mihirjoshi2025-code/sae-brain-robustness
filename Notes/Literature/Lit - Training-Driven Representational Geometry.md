# Lit — Training-Driven Representational Geometry

> [!summary] One line
> Shows how a model's **representational geometry** becomes modular across training, using [[Pythia|Pythia]] checkpoints — relevant to the **stretch goal**.

## What it's about
- Uses the open Pythia checkpoint suite (many saved steps) to watch representations reorganize during training.
- "Modularization" = distinct capabilities becoming separable in representation space over time.

## How it maps to our project
- Methodological blueprint for the **checkpoint stretch goal**: Pythia gives many public checkpoints, so we could measure alignment/geometry across training without training anything ourselves.
- Pairs with [[Lit - From Language to Cognition (AlKhamissi)]].
