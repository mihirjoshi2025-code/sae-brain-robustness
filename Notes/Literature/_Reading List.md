# Reading List

**Everyone reads the first three before kickoff.** Owners read the rest relevant to their workstream. 

## Read before kickoff (everyone)
1. [[Lit - Cortical Semantic Topography (Guo, Wu, Yiu)]] — *Sparse Autoencoders Map Brain–LLM Alignment onto Cortical Semantic Topography.* Source of **claims 2 & 3**.
2. [[Lit - Sparse Features for Brain Responses (Lepori, Kay, Tuckute)]] — *Interpreting Brain Responses to Language with Sparse Features from Language Models.* Source of **claims 1 & 4**.
3. [[Lit - What Are LLMs Mapping To (Feghhi, Hadidi)]] — *What Are Large Language Models Mapping to in the Brain?* Why our **controls** matter.

## Read by relevant owners
4. [[Lit - Illusions of Alignment]] — follow-up critique of brain scores. (Controls & statistics)
5. [[Lit - Back to the Feature (ICLR 2026)]] — SAE approach on intracranial recordings. (Feature extraction / taxonomy)
6. [[Lit - From Language to Cognition (AlKhamissi)]] — alignment across training; for the **stretch goal**. (Lead)
7. [[Lit - Training-Driven Representational Geometry]] — alignment & geometry across Pythia checkpoints. (Lead / stretch goal)

## How to take notes
Each lit note has: **one-line claim**, **what they did**, **what they found**, **how it maps to our claims/controls**, and **what's shaky**. Keep it plain-English; link jargon to [[_Vocab Index]].
