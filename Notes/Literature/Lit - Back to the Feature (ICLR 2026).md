# Lit — Back to the Feature (ICLR 2026)

> [!summary] One line
> Applies the [[Sparse Autoencoder (SAE)|SAE]]-feature approach to **intracranial** recordings (electrodes on/in the brain) instead of fMRI — higher temporal resolution.

## Why it's useful to us
- Shows the SAE-feature method generalizes beyond [[fMRI]] to **intracranial** data.
- Intracranial recordings have **much better time resolution** than fMRI, which averages over ~2s and blurs several words together (a known fMRI limitation). Useful context for interpreting what our fMRI results can and can't resolve.
- Methodological reference for the **Feature extraction** and **Feature taxonomy** workstreams: how others extract and interpret SAE features against neural data.

## How it maps to our project
Not a source of our four claims, but a **methods benchmark**. If a claim holds in fMRI *and* appears in intracranial work, that's stronger cross-method evidence. Keep as supporting literature for the write-up.
